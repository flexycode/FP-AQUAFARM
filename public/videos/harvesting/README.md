# Harvesting Videos

Place your 3 harvesting video files here with these exact filenames:

- harvest-video-1.mp4  (your ~0:58 video)
- harvest-video-2.mp4  (your ~1:01 video)
- harvest-video-3.mp4  (your ~0:50 video)

## Compression Tips

Before adding, compress with HandBrake or FFmpeg:
- Codec: H.264 (MP4)
- Resolution: 1080p or 720p
- Bitrate: 2-4 Mbps
- Audio: Strip entirely (auto-play muted)
- Target: Under 15 MB per clip
