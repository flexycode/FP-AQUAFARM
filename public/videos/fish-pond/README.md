# Fish Pond Videos

Place your 2 fish pond video files here with these exact filenames:

- pond-video-1.mp4  (your 0:14 video)
- pond-video-2.mp4  (your 0:20 video)

## Compression Tips

Before adding, compress with HandBrake or FFmpeg:
- Codec: H.264 (MP4)
- Resolution: 1080p or 720p
- Bitrate: 2-4 Mbps
- Audio: Strip entirely (auto-play muted)
- Target: Under 10 MB per clip for short videos
